Who We Are

We are a New Delhi production studio that believes every frame should earn its place on screen.

Half Baked Studio was born from the conviction that great stories don't need enormous budgets — they need the right creative team. Founded by Arjit Jha, we've built our reputation on delivering cinematic quality for brands of every size: from emerging D2C startups looking for their first ad film, to established companies refreshing their visual identity.

Our work spans 30-second performance ads engineered for conversion, festival-calibre short films, platform-native reels and social content that builds communities rather than just chasing impressions. Every project starts with a question: What does this story need to become unforgettable?
